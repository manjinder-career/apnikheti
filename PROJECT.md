PROJECT NAME:
Apni Kheti Apne Beej — ਆਪਣੀ ਖੇਤੀ ਆਪਣੇ ਬੀਜ

PROJECT TYPE:
A complete Laravel-based website dedicated to traditional seeds,
seed conservation, organic farming, natural farming, and farmer education.

==================================================
1. PROJECT BACKGROUND
==================================================

Create a professional website for the initiative "Apni Kheti Apne Beej",
associated with Amrit Singh Chahal.

Amrit Singh Chahal works with various organizations and promotes
traditional seeds, seed conservation, and sustainable agricultural
practices.

The initiative is associated with the preservation and promotion of
traditional seed varieties. According to the project brief, more than
500 traditional seeds and varieties have been collected or documented.

The website should communicate the importance of:

- Traditional and indigenous seeds.
- Preservation of agricultural biodiversity.
- Organic and natural farming.
- Farmers' rights to preserve and exchange seeds.
- Traditional farming knowledge.
- Chemical-free and sustainable agricultural practices.
- Farmer-to-farmer knowledge sharing.
- Local food systems and food sovereignty.
- Protecting traditional agricultural heritage for future generations.

The slogan is:

"Apni Kheti Apne Beej"
"Our Farming, Our Seeds"

Punjabi slogan:
"ਆਪਣੀ ਖੇਤੀ ਆਪਣੇ ਬੀਜ"

Build a platform that can grow over time into a comprehensive
knowledge base for traditional seeds and sustainable farming.

IMPORTANT:
Treat the information about the founder, organizations, seed
collection, and achievements as preliminary information supplied
by the project owner.

Do not invent awards, qualifications, partnerships, historical
claims, or achievements.

Make the founder's biography, organization details, and seed statistics
editable through the admin panel.

5. SEED CONTRIBUTION SYSTEM
==================================================

Create a form that allows farmers, researchers, and seed savers
to submit information about traditional seeds.

Form fields:

- Contributor name.
- Village.
- District.
- State.
- Seed name in Punjabi.
- Seed name in English, if known.
- Crop category.
- Alternative local names.
- Description.
- Traditional uses.
- Growing season.
- Seed preservation method.
- How long the variety has been known to the contributor.
- Photographs.
- Optional supporting documents.
- Optional contact details.
- Consent to process and review the submission.
- Permission preferences for publicly displaying the contribution.

Submission workflow:

1. A visitor submits seed information.
2. Laravel validates the data.
3. The submission is saved with pending status.
4. An administrator reviews the information.
5. The administrator may request corrections.
6. The administrator verifies sources and relevant details.
7. The administrator approves, rejects, or archives the submission.
8. Approved information can be published in the seed library.

Prevent spam and malicious uploads.

Validate image types and file sizes.

Store original submissions separately from published seed records.

Do not automatically publish unverified agricultural information.
